- Description : Guess the mystery perfume of the day in 6 tries. Drawn from the 100 best-known perfumes. Free, no sign-up, in English and French.

## Show HN (news.ycombinator.com, pas de karma requis)
Titre : Show HN: Perfumdle, a daily perfume guessing game with notes as clues
URL : https://perfumdle.com/en/game/
Texte :
I built a daily guessing game around perfume. The mystery perfume comes from the 100 most searched ones. Clues unlock one by one (family, base note, heart note, decade, gender, top note, house initial). Each guess is compared on house, year, gender and family, plus a count of shared notes, which turned out to be the clue people use most.
It sits on top of a bilingual encyclopedia of 307 perfumes (full note pyramids, similar perfumes computed from shared notes). Static Astro site on Cloudflare Pages, no account, no tracking beyond Plausible.
Happy to hear what makes it too hard or too easy.

## Réponses sur Reddit / Quora (sans lien les 2 à 3 premières semaines)
Répondre aux questions « what smells like X », « what does oud smell like », « dupe for Y » avec une vraie réponse :
2 ou 3 parfums proches et pourquoi (notes communes). Le lien vers la page correspondante seulement quand il apporte
quelque chose (liste complète, pyramide), et en précisant que c'est ton site.
Exemple (r/fragrance, question « something like Baccarat Rouge 540 but less sweet? ») :
BR540 is mostly ambroxan, saffron and a sugary jasmine. If the sweetness is the problem, look at scents built on the same
ambery-woody base with less sugar: Molecule 01 is almost pure Iso E Super and reads as a quieter skin scent, and Oud Satin Mood
keeps the same house signature but goes darker. Trying them on skin matters more than any list.
