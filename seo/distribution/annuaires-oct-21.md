URL : https://perfumdle.com/en/game/
Premier commentaire (à poster juste après) :
I built this because perfume talk is all about notes, and I wanted a game where the notes are the clues. Every day there is one mystery perfume from the 100 most searched. A miss unlocks a clue and each guess shows the notes you share with the answer.
The site is static (Astro on Cloudflare Pages). The data covers 307 perfumes, and I used it for a small study of which notes rose and fell by decade: oakmoss went from 31% to 2% of perfumes, ambroxan from 0 to 19%. https://perfumdle.com/en/fragrance-notes-by-decade/
Happy to hear what makes the clues too easy or too hard.
