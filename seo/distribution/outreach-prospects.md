# Perfumdle: free editorial link prospects

Research date: 2026-10-10. Web research only: nobody was contacted and no form was submitted.
"Alive" means a post or upload in the last 12 months (after 2025-10-10). I loaded every site listed below. YouTube data comes from the channel pages and RSS feeds. Substack dates come from each newsletter's RSS feed.

Hook URLs are on perfumdle.com. I checked that each one returned HTTP 200 on 2026-10-10, including `/fr/test-parfum/`, which already responds.

Legend: bucket 1 = blog/newsletter, 2 = resource list, 3 = daily-games list, 4 = creator.
[!] = something is uncertain. The note next to it says what.

## Competitive intel found along the way
Other perfume guessing games exist:
- Scentle (scentle.live, scentle.cc, scentmap.co/scentle)
- Fragriddle (fragriddle.com, 5 modes)
- Eauxle (github visvaya/fragrance-game)
- Stinkle (Basenotes thread)
- SCENTLE by Fragrapedia (weekly)

None of them appear on the directories above yet, so being listed first on the FR directories (jeux-du-jour, 1jour1jeu) is a real edge. The FR-first, bilingual angle is the differentiator to pitch.
